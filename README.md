# **CyberSecurity Journey**

---

## **From Soldier to Analyst**

While serving in the United States Army, I was involved in a severe car accident that changed the trajectory of my life. Even after leaving active duty, I knew I still wanted to serve — just in a different way. That realization opened the door to cybersecurity, where I found a new mission and a new purpose.

---

## **A New Path Forward**

I transitioned from being an Infantry Drill Sergeant to becoming a Digital Forensics Analyst, trading in field manuals for hex editors and evidence drives. Along the way, I discovered that not only do I have a passion for this industry, but I thrive under pressure and genuinely enjoy the challenge. In cybersecurity, no two problems are ever the same, and that constant evolution keeps me sharp and motivated.
